# Support

Welcome to Game2048 support! This page will help you get started and find answers to common questions.

## How to Play

1. **Move Tiles**: Swipe on the board (iOS) or press arrow keys (Mac) to slide all tiles
2. **Merge Numbers**: When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one
3. **Reach 2048**: Keep merging to create the 2048 tile — then go beyond

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I control the game?
On iPhone and iPad, swipe left, right, up, or down on the board. On Mac, use the arrow keys.

### Does this app require any permissions?
No. Game2048 works without any special permissions.

### Will I lose my progress if I close the app?
Your best score is saved automatically and persists between sessions. The current game will be lost if you close the app.

### Can I undo a move?
Undo is not currently supported. Plan each move carefully!

### I reached 2048. What now?
A victory screen will appear. You can either start a new game or keep going to push your score even higher.
